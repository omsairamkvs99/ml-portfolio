03

Instrument readings

Four resources set the price of a model: data, compute, energy, and time, with people running through all of them. Each one also shapes how capable the final model becomes.

Resource 1

Data

Models learn from text measured in tokens, pieces of words. Training sets grew about fifty times in four years, and the best text is increasingly licensed rather than free.

Effect on the model: more and cleaner data improves knowledge and reasoning, and the mix decides what the model is good at.

Data also costs money directly: OpenAI's 2024 content deal with News Corp was reported to be worth more than $250 million over five years.

Resource 2

Compute

Training compute is roughly 6 × parameters × tokens floating point operations, run on thousands of GPUs or TPUs wired together. The chips are the single largest line item.

Effect on the model: more compute allows bigger models trained on more data, the main driver of capability gains.

Amortized costs for frontier models such as GPT-4 and Gemini (Cottier et al., 2024).

Resource 3

Energy

Every GPU draws hundreds of watts around the clock, plus cooling. Energy is a small share of the dollar cost but the most visible environmental cost, and it is growing fastest in absolute terms.

Effect on the model: energy does not make a model smarter by itself, but power and cooling limits now decide how large a cluster can be built.

Resource 4

Time and people

A frontier run ties up a cluster for weeks to months, and hardware fails constantly at that scale. Around the run sit researchers, engineers, and human raters whose salaries rival the chip bill.

Effect on the model: longer runs on more data improve quality, while human feedback turns a raw text predictor into a helpful assistant.

Signal strength

How much each developer has revealed about training. Only Meta transmits clearly. For the rest, the numbers come from outside estimates or a single comment, so read them as faint signals.

06

Open questions

The numbers so far point toward a future no one has measured yet.

?01
When does the data run out?

Models may be trained on the entire stock of public human text sometime between 2026 and 2032.

Villalobos et al., 2024
?02
Who can afford the next one?

At 2.4× a year, the largest training runs pass $1 billion by 2027, leaving very few organizations able to build at the frontier.

Cottier et al., 2024
?03
Where will the power come from?

Energy is only 2 to 6% of the cost, yet power and cooling now decide where clusters can be built at all. Grok 4 alone is estimated at 310 GWh.

Cottier et al., 2024; Epoch AI, 2025
?04
How much stays hidden?

Of the seven frontier models charted here, only Meta's come with full training numbers. The rest are estimates or comments.
